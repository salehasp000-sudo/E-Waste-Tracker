Every device follows a strict forward-only lifecycle recorded immutably on Ethereum. No record can ever be deleted or altered.

Core Features

Everything You Need

⬡

Immutable Ledger

All device records are stored on Ethereum smart contracts. Zero tampering possible after confirmation.

◉

QR Code Linking

Every device gets a unique QR code at registration, physically bridging the digital and physical world.

🦊

MetaMask Auth

No passwords. No databases. Users authenticate via their Ethereum wallet. Fully decentralized identity.

◈

IPFS Document Storage

Supporting documents are stored on IPFS — content-addressed and permanent, referenced by hash.

⚿

Role-Based Access

Manufacturers, consumers, collectors, recyclers, and regulators each get precisely scoped permissions.

📈

Regulatory Dashboard

Government agencies view verified e-waste data with filters by date, device type, and recycler.
