Seven steps, one direction

A device only moves forward, one step at a time. Each step is stamped with who made it and when.

  1. 1

    Registered

    The manufacturer records the device and its serial number.

  2. 2

    QR generated

    A unique QR code is tied to the device on-chain.

  3. 3

    Sold with consumer

    The device reaches its first consumer owner.

  4. 4

    Collected

    A collector takes it in at end of life.

  5. 5

    Processing

    A certified recycler starts dismantling it.

  6. 6

    Recycled or disposed

    The recycler records the outcome and attaches proof.

  7. 7

    Closed

    An admin closes the record.

Who does what

Permissions are enforced by the smart contract, not by this website. A wallet can only do what its role allows.
